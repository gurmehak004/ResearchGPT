Foundational Motive

Bridging the Gap in Scholarly Comprehension

01

The Literature Velocity Crisis

With thousands of preprints published weekly, scholars spend up to 70% of their research cycle merely locating relevant ablation tables and architectural configurations rather than conducting novel inquiry.

02

The Unreliability of Generative AI

Standard language models routinely hallucinate non-existent authors, skew statistical findings, and blend disparate studies. Academic workflows demand deterministic grounding where every statement is traceable to an exact paragraph.

03

Isolated Context Silos

Conventional readers restrict analysis to a single document view. ResearchGPT eliminates context fragmentation, allowing researchers to query entire thematic paper clusters as a unified, interactive knowledge corpus.
